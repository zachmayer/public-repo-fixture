# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing. To keep changes easy to review:

1. **Keep changes focused.** Make each pull request address one issue or one logical change, and describe what it does and why in the PR description.
2. **Check your work before opening a pull request.** Run the existing tests and linters, and confirm that your change works as described.
3. **Be respectful and constructive in discussions.** Assume good intent, explain your reasoning, and be open to feedback on your changes.
