# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing. To keep changes easy to review:

1. **Keep changes focused.** Make one logical change per commit and per pull request, and describe what changed and why in the pull request description.
2. **Check your work before opening a pull request.** Run the existing tests and linters, and confirm that your change works as described.
3. **Be respectful in discussions.** Give constructive feedback on code, not on people, and be open to revising your approach based on review.
