# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing! Please follow these guidelines:

1. **Keep changes focused.** Make one logical change per pull request, and describe what it does and why in the PR description.
2. **Test your changes.** Verify that your change works as expected and does not break existing behaviour before requesting review.
3. **Be respectful in discussion.** Give constructive feedback on code and proposals, and assume good intent from other contributors.
